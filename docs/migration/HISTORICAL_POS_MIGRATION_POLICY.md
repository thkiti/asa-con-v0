# Historical POS Migration Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the accounting policy used when migrating historical POS transactions
(January–May 2026) into ASA-CON.

The objective is to preserve transaction-level accounting integrity while reconciling with
legacy monthly accounting records.

---

## Background

Legacy accounting records were produced using monthly aggregated calculations.

ASA-CON performs accounting per individual transaction.

Therefore, small rounding differences are expected.

Typical variance:

- +0.01
- -0.03
- +0.06

These differences are caused solely by rounding methodology,
not by accounting errors.

---

## Accounting Principle

ASA-CON is the accounting source of truth.

All accounting postings are generated from individual business transactions.

```
Receipt
  ↓
Voucher
  ↓
Journal Entry
  ↓
General Ledger
```

Financial statements are always derived from posted journals.

The system never recalculates accounting from monthly totals
simply to eliminate rounding differences.

---

## VAT Policy

VAT is calculated and rounded for every transaction.

Example:

| | Amount |
|---|------:|
| Sales Amount | 100.00 |
| VAT | 6.54 |
| Revenue | 93.46 |

This is preferred over calculating VAT from the monthly sales total.

---

## Migration Reconciliation

Historical posting is executed using transaction-level accounting.

After posting, Accounting compares:

**Manual monthly report** vs **ASA-CON monthly report**

The comparison is performed per month.

Example:

| Month | Manual | ASA-CON | Variance |
|-------|-------:|--------:|---------:|
| 2026-01 | xxx | xxx | +0.06 |
| 2026-02 | xxx | xxx | -0.01 |
| 2026-03 | xxx | xxx | +0.03 |
| 2026-04 | xxx | xxx | -0.08 |
| 2026-05 | xxx | xxx | +0.02 |

---

## Rounding Adjustment

If variance exists,
Finance prepares one Manual Journal Voucher (MJV)
per accounting period.

Example:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Document | `MJV-ROUND-202601` |
| Description | Historical POS Migration — Transaction-level Rounding Adjustment |
| Reference | Historical POS Backfill |
| Purpose | Adjust legacy monthly rounding differences only |

This adjustment is **not** a correction of accounting errors.

It documents the difference between:

- Legacy monthly aggregate calculations
- ASA-CON transaction-level accounting

---

## Frequency

One adjustment per accounting month.

**Not** one adjustment for the entire migration.

Reasons:

- easier audit
- monthly TB reconciliation
- monthly P&L reconciliation
- monthly Balance Sheet reconciliation
- cleaner audit trail

---

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **ASA-CON** | Generates accounting entries correctly per transaction |
| **Accounting Staff** | Performs reconciliation against legacy reports |
| **Finance** | Reviews variance; approves monthly rounding adjustment |
| **Auditor** | Reviews reconciliation and adjustment evidence |

---

## Scope

This policy applies **only** to the historical migration period:

- January 2026
- February 2026
- March 2026
- April 2026
- May 2026

After full ASA-CON go-live,
no monthly rounding adjustment should normally be required because
all accounting originates from ASA-CON transactions.

## Materiality

Historical migration rounding variances are expected because the legacy accounting system calculated VAT and revenue from monthly aggregate totals, while ASA-CON posts accounting per individual transaction.

These variances are normally immaterial and are not considered accounting errors.

Typical examples:

- +0.01
- -0.03
- +0.06

Finance reviews the monthly reconciliation report before preparing the monthly adjustment.

Where necessary, one Manual Journal Voucher (MJV) is prepared per accounting month:

Document:

`MJV-ROUND-YYYYMM`

Purpose:

Historical POS Migration – Transaction-level Rounding Adjustment.

The adjustment documents the difference between:

- Legacy monthly aggregate calculations
- ASA-CON transaction-level accounting

It does NOT correct system errors.

---

## Auditor Considerations

The migration policy intentionally preserves transaction-level accounting.

The project does not modify individual historical receipts or vouchers merely to eliminate immaterial rounding differences.

Instead, reconciliation is performed monthly and any immaterial variance is documented through a dedicated monthly MJV.

This approach provides:

- Complete audit trail
- Transparent reconciliation
- Monthly traceability
- Consistent accounting architecture
- Financial statements derived from posted journals rather than recalculated monthly totals

---

## Design Decision

ASA-CON permanently adopts **transaction-level accounting** as the accounting source of truth.

Financial statements are always produced from posted Journal Entries.

The system intentionally does NOT recalculate accounting values from monthly totals simply to eliminate rounding differences.

Monthly rounding adjustments are considered a migration-only process and apply only to the historical migration period (January–May 2026).

After full ASA-CON go-live, this adjustment process should normally no longer be required.
